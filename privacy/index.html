What SmartBuzz uses, and why

Your location

Location Alert and Don't Forget tell you when you arrive at or leave places you have saved. To do this, SmartBuzz uses Apple's region monitoring, which lets iOS notice when you cross the edge of a saved place, even when the app is closed. This is why the app asks for location access “Always”.

  • Your location is processed on your iPhone only. It is never sent to us or to any third party, and we keep no history of where you have been.
  • The places you save (name, map position, radius and icon) are stored only on your iPhone.
  • You can change or turn off location access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › SmartBuzz. Location-based alerts will stop working without it.

Battery level

Charge Alert reads your iPhone's battery level and charging state while the feature is open, so it can alert you at the level you chose. This reading is used immediately on your iPhone and is not stored or shared.

Notifications

SmartBuzz uses local notifications, which are created and delivered by your iPhone itself. No notification content passes through our servers.

Shortcuts

If you set up the optional Shortcuts automation for Charge Alert, it runs on your iPhone through Apple's Shortcuts app. SmartBuzz receives no information about your other shortcuts.

Your reminders and settings

Your alerts, checklists, chosen theme and other settings are stored only on your iPhone. If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or a computer, this data may be included in that backup, which is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal data, usage data, device identifiers or crash reports.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking, and we don't sell or share data.
  • SmartBuzz 1.0 doesn't send any information to us or to third-party services.

Deleting your data

Everything SmartBuzz stores is on your iPhone. You can delete individual places and checklists in the app, or remove all of it by deleting the app.

Children

SmartBuzz isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law (UK GDPR and GDPR) you have rights over your personal data, including access, correction and deletion. Because SmartBuzz doesn't collect or hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access or delete, but you're welcome to contact us with any questions. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If SmartBuzz changes how it handles data (for example, if a future version adds optional sign-in and syncing), we'll update this policy before that version is released and change the “Last updated” date above.
